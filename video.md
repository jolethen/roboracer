# Video Production & Scene-by-Scene Execution Guide

### 1. Cinematic Teaser (0:00 - 0:15)
* **Visual:** Low-angle, slow-motion panning shots focusing on the wheels spinning, the chassis design, and the glowing LED on the ESP-12E board.
* **Audio / Action:** Upbeat background music kicks in immediately with no dead air. Quick cuts build excitement right from the start.

### 2. Team Introductions (0:15 - 0:30)
* **Visual:** Quick, energetic 5-second clips of each team member holding relevant hardware or tools (e.g., holding the phone controller, soldering iron, or chassis).
* **Audio / Action:** On-screen text overlays show names and roles while members wave or give a quick thumbs-up to the camera.

### 3. Hardware & Architecture Breakdown (0:30 - 1:30)
* **Visual:** Clear close-ups of the internal layout. Point the camera directly at the ESP-12E NodeMCU, the motor driver, the LM2596 buck converter, and the star ground bus.
* **Audio / Action:** Voiceover or on-camera explanation detailing:
  * How the ESP-12E hosts its own standalone Wi-Fi Access Point (SoftAP) for zero router dependency and low latency.
  * Why the buck converter is used to step down the 3S battery voltage to a stable 5V for logic components.
  * How the motor driver handles power distribution to the dual motors and how star grounding prevents signal noise.

### 4. Work-In-Progress (WIP) Montage (1:30 - 2:00)
* **Visual:** Fast-paced montage of behind-the-scenes engineering work. Show typing code in the Arduino IDE, testing circuits on a breadboard, and assembling parts in the workshop.
* **Audio / Action:** Fast-forwarded background audio or upbeat cuts showing the iterative building and debugging process.

### 5. Live Driving Demonstration (2:00 - 2:50)
* **Visual:** Split-screen or alternating clips showing a finger tapping the custom web controller UI on a smartphone screen, immediately followed by the physical robot reacting on the floor.
* **Audio / Action:** Footage of the robot successfully navigating different track environments (the computer lab, school hallways, and the outdoor ground) to prove connection stability and maneuverability across surfaces.

### 6. Outro & Credits (2:50 - 3:00)
* **Visual:** Clean closing screen displaying the project name, GitHub repo link, and team member credits.
* **Audio / Action:** Music fades out cleanly as the video concludes.
